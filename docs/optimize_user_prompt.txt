What's Actually Needed
The orchestrator needs to make pedagogical decisions, so it requires:

Essential:

Confidence level (affects teaching strategy)
Flow score (affects whether to interrupt/correct)
Recent error rate (helps prioritize what to address)
Grammar/vocabulary mastery (helps decide what to teach)
Debatable:

Topic (could be inferred from conversation)
Turn number (only matters for very short conversations)
Conversation summary (LLM can see the history)
Optimization Options
